FAQ

Common questions

What does it cost?

Pricing is invoiced per submission on a Starter or Pro plan, agreed with you directly — there's no fixed public list price yet. Request access and we'll confirm your rate before you're live.

Do I have to be D&TA-accredited to use this?

No. Any D&T or workshop safety course can run through the portal. D&TA accreditation support is there if your course needs it, not a requirement to sign up.

What happens to my delegates' data?

Only what's needed to run the course is collected, and you can turn off home-address collection for D&TA-accredited courses since D&TA already holds it. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How long does setup take?

A registration request is usually reviewed and approved within a day or two. Once approved, your portal is ready as soon as you set a password.

Can more than one trainer at my organisation use it?

Each trainer registers their own portal today. If you need several trainers under one organisation, mention it when you request access and we'll talk through the best setup.

Running your own in-house H&S training? We're developing a version of this for schools to track their own students' health & safety training directly — not started yet, but if you'd like to be first to know, get in touch.
